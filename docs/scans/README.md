# Scans of the original sources

Page images from the two sources, reduced in size. They are here so that
the transcription in `programs/compiler-1951.bohm` and the corrections in
`bohm errata` can be checked against what was actually printed.

| Folder | Source |
|--------|--------|
| `boehm-1954/` | Corrado Böhm, *Calculatrices digitales. Du déchiffrage de formules logico-mathématiques par la machine même dans la conception du programme*, Bologna 1954. From the ETH Zürich research collection. Files are named by the printed page number. |
| `knuth-trabb-pardo-1976/` | Donald E. Knuth and Luis Trabb Pardo, *The Early Development of Programming Languages*, Stanford report STAN-CS-76-562, August 1976. From bitsavers.org. Files are named by the page of the PDF, not the printed page number. |
| `details/` | Crops of the passages quoted most. |

Where to look:

- The title page: `boehm-1954/page-01.jpg`.
- Euclid's algorithm, section 3.4: `boehm-1954/page-25.jpg`.
- The compiler, chapter 7: pages 39, 40, 41 and 43. Group C, with the
  choice that picks two groups at once, is on page 40. Group E′, which
  leaves out the operation code, is on page 43.
- Knuth and Trabb Pardo on Böhm: `knuth-trabb-pardo-1976/`. The remark
  about the bug is on their printed page 40 and the formula worked by hand
  on page 41 (`details/knuth-bug.jpg`, `details/knuth-table.jpg`).

These are not the whole of either work. Both are copyright their authors
and publishers; the pages are reproduced for reference and research. The
complete documents are linked from the main README.
